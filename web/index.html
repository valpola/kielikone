        Instructions
        

          

            Type your answer (optional), press Enter or click the "Show Answer" button to reveal, then
            Enter again to auto-grade. You can also grade manually with the buttons.
          

          
Do I need an account?

          

            No. Your answers are always kept on this device, and the practice set is built
            from them, so everything works without logging in — it just stays in this browser
            and is lost if you clear its data.
          

          

            Logging in stores your history centrally instead, so it follows you to other
            devices. The button in the corner shows your name once you are logged in; press it
            to enter your app secret, which is remembered on this device.

Filtering

          

            A word is quizzed only if it has every Include tag and
            none of the Exclude tags. So ticking two units asks for words
            belonging to both, which is usually nothing. Ticked tags are listed first, under
            "Selected", and the number beside each tag is how many words carry it.
            The filter applies immediately to the quiz.
          

          
Special "Practice set" tag

          

            "Recompute practice set" picks the words worth practising next, based on how you
            have answered them before, and tags them "Practice set". Words you got wrong come
            back soonest; words you have never seen are nudged up so they surface at all.
            "Words per practice set" controls how many are picked.
          

          

            Starting fresh there is no set yet, so you are quizzed on the whole vocabulary.
            Answer a few words, then recompute. The set is worked out on this device and
            stored here: it never travels with the vocabulary and never affects your other
            devices, so each device can be working on its own batch.
          

          
What the numbers mean

          

            After a recompute, Options shows something like
            1823 words in filter · avg -0.505 · max 2.000. The first is how many
            words your current tag filters allow. The other two are the priority score,
            averaged over those words and at its highest.
          

          

            A higher score means a word is more in need of practice. Three anchors make the
            rest readable:
          

          
    
            
  • 2.0 — you just got it wrong
    • 
            
  • 1.0 — you have never been asked it
    • 
            
  • -0.5 — you just got it right
    • 
          

          

            Scores drift back toward zero as time passes, a wrong answer fading more slowly
            than a right one, so a word you missed weeks ago still outranks one you answered
            correctly yesterday. An average below zero means most of the words in your filter
            are in good shape.
          

          

            The line below it describes your stored history: how many answers are cached on
            this device, and — only when the two disagree — how many more or fewer the
            database holds. Answers from your other devices are picked up automatically, so
            normally these agree. A difference means something arrived out of order, usually an
            answer that waited on a bad connection before syncing, or that an undo has just
            removed a row this device still has cached. Either way the next recompute reloads
            the full history and the numbers settle.
          

          
Session target

          

            "Correct to finish" controls how many correct answers are required before a
            word is skipped for the rest of the session. The correct answers are reset when
            the practice set is recomputed or when the page is reloaded.
